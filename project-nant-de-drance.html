Hydro Electric Pump Storage Plant Nant de Drance.

Claim and Contract Manager General Contractor side

Period
2013-2014
Project costs
approx. CHF 1.5 billion (shell construction with dam heightening approx. CHF 800 million)
Client
Nant de Drance SA, Finhaut, Switzerland
Location
Finhaut, Switzerland

The project

More than 400 workers, about 10 years of underground construction work in the high mountains, 17 km of tunnels and 1.7 million cubic metres of excavated rock: the Nant de Drance construction site was gigantic.

The result is one of the most powerful pumped storage power plants in Europe. With a capacity of 900 MW, it will generate around 2.5 billion kWh of electricity per year. Testing of the wet parts will start in 2019 and commissioning will take place gradually from 2020.

Challenges

In order to be able to use the pumped storage power plant as flexibly as possible, the storage capacity of the Vieux-Emosson reservoir was doubled. To achieve this, the existing dam had to be raised by 20 metres. A total of 68,000 cubic metres of concrete, distributed over 475 blocks, each one and a half metres high and twelve metres long on each side, were placed for the increase. In order to create a solid foundation, the existing 45 metre high wall was partially removed.

My role in the project

Claim and Contract Manager General Contractor side, responsible for:

  • Development of a supplementary claim management system and contract negotiation and management
  • Quality assurance and optimization of various construction processes
  • Risk and opportunity management
  • Complete supplementary claim management and negotiations
