FIDIC Claim Management.

The workshop “FIDIC Claim Management” is exactly tailored to your daily international project business.

The basic part of the workshop enables you to understand claim management even better on the basis of practical examples, to put it into context and to apply it convincingly. The second part is dedicated to real examples from various international projects, demonstrating different claim approaches and how they can be successfully implemented.

Within group exercises you develop and discuss solutions for claim settings and claim defence as they occur in your daily project routine, and learn how to deal with them better and therefore more efficiently.

Content of the workshop

In this workshop you learn to understand claim management as a component of project management. You learn which strategies you can use to assert your claims more successfully or to defend against unjustified claims more effectively. After the workshop you are able to introduce claim strategies in your company or to optimize existing claim management procedures.

  • Definition of claim versus claim management
  • Basics of FIDIC
  • Explanation of FIDIC-based contracts
  • Goals of claim management
  • Handling of claims
  • Claim management process (aggressive versus defensive claim management)
  • The role of a contract and claim manager
  • Risk management in projects
  • Identification and documentation of deviations during the course of the project
  • Possible economic consequences of deviations and amendments to contracts
  • Conducting claim-relevant correspondence in due form and time
  • Claim avoidance through efficient project and contract management
  • Development of claim strategies based on corporate culture
  • Best practice examples
  • Disturbances in construction progress versus disturbed construction progress
  • Proper handling of claims for extension of the construction period

Audience

This workshop is aimed at everyone who sets or defends claims: project managers, site managers, key account managers, specialists and executives in execution, sales and purchasing. You benefit not only from the input of an experienced contract and claim manager but also from the experience of the other participants, and expand your professional network.

About the speaker

For more than 25 years Michael Becker has been confronted with claims in the national and international construction business and in many different kinds of projects. At the Gotthard Base Tunnel project, he and his team successfully fended off a mega claim from an international consortium of contractors (the original wording on the existing site mentions “less than 85 % of the claim amount originally submitted”).
