Reconstruction of Kaiserlei traffic circle.

Project Management utility construction

Period
2021-2022
Project costs
± EUR 60 million
Client
City of Offenbach, Germany
Location
Offenbach, Germany

The project

The reconstruction at the Kaiserlei traffic circle means the end for what was once Europe’s largest traffic circle, which will be replaced by intersections, and is the start for the further development of the entire area.

The reconstruction of the Kaiserlei traffic circle will separate the various traffic flows in the future: development traffic in the neighbourhood, traffic between Frankfurt and Offenbach, and feeder traffic to and from the A661 freeway. Subsequently, the City of Offenbach will have additional land to expand the commercial location.

Challenges

Taking on an ongoing project as an external consultant requires the highest level of experience and commitment within a complex environment with many project participants and interests. Consistent project management and binding communication generates significant benefit for the customer and saves costs.

My role in the project

Project Management utility construction, responsible for:

  • Project team support and impulse
  • Implementation of supplementary claim processes
  • Increase of binding communication to all project participants
  • Project, Contract and Claims Management until acceptance and commissioning
