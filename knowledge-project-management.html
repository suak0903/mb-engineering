Project Management for Large-Scale Projects.

Solid project management is like the oil in the gearbox of an engine.

The better you plan and manage your projects, the smoother they run, and the less time and money they take.

Managing projects is a major factor in the success of every company. To be successful, the project environment must be analysed clearly and influenced specifically, using proven project management concepts and techniques. Using the example of project management at the Gotthard Base Tunnel, state-of-the-art PM methods are presented according to the key objective “lessons learned”.

Content of the workshop

In this workshop you learn to understand project management on the basis of a mega project. The PM methods acquired here can be applied to any of your projects. After the workshop you are able to introduce project management processes in your company or to optimize existing ones.

  • Project and project management functions
  • PM process methods at a glance
  • Basics of large-scale project management
  • Definition of scope and content of project management
  • PM processes and procedure for implementation
  • Creation of a project structure organisation
  • Control and processes in project management as a life-cycle key objective
  • Project closure

Audience

This workshop is suitable for young professionals in project management, for experienced project managers as a refresher, and for internal and external organizational consultants from specialist departments who are directly or indirectly involved in projects.

About the speaker

Michael Becker has gained experience in national and international construction for more than 25 years and, as former Head of Contract Management IG GBTS at the Gotthard Base Tunnel project, worked for the client during the finalization of the shell construction and shell equipment phase in the Sedrun, Faido and Bodio lots. He holds the certification Certified Senior Project Manager IAPM and keeps abreast of the latest PM developments through regular further training.
